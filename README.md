# EDIPE_shinyapp
The European Diatom Inventory for Palaeoecology and Ecology (EDIPE) is a a curated bibliographic resource of diatom studies encompassing modern ecological surveys, surface sediments, top-bottom samples, and sediment core records (1965-2025). 

🌐 The objective is to enhance diatom data discoverability, accessibility, and reuse across ecological and palaeoecological domains in continental and island environments (freshwater and brackish).

🖥 This R shiny app allows users to query the inventory via filtering and visualizing where the diatom collection units are and their temporal age span, as well as  associated metadata. 

🚀 Launch the app here: https://edipe.shinyapps.io/shiny/ 

---

##Features
- Search publications by site name, article title, ecosystem type, dating method
- Filter collection units by:
  - Data source: European Diatom Database (EDDI), LakeCores metadatabase, and novel literature review PalaeOpen
  - Data type: sediment surface sample, top-bottom core, core, and section 
  - Data available and repository
  - Temporal coverage

## Interactive visualizations
- Geographic distribution of studies
- Temporal distribution of collection units by time bins
- Raw data explorer
- Download filtered records as CSV 
- Export figures

## 📊 Data sources
  
The EDIPE database compiles published diatom literature from multiple disciplinary and regional sources. Records are curated and standardized to facilitate bibliographic searches and large-scale assessments of research coverage across Europe.
